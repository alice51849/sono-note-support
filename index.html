Support & FAQ

Need help? We're happy to assist.

How does recording work?

Tap record, speak naturally, then stop when you're done. Sono Note creates a transcript and turns it into a summary, action items, and an optional follow-up email draft.

Why is transcription sometimes imperfect?

Speech recognition can be affected by background noise, accents, multiple speakers, distance from the microphone, technical terms, and language availability. Clear, single-speaker recordings work best. Generated output should be reviewed before use.

Does transcription happen on device?

On-device Apple Speech recognition is preferred when supported. For some languages, devices, or recognition modes, Apple may use network-based speech recognition, and iOS will ask for permission.

Which languages are supported?

Sono Note is designed with first-class quality for Traditional Chinese (Taiwan) and English, works well for Japanese and Korean, and offers a natural experience in many more languages. Availability and quality may vary by device, iOS version, and Apple Speech support.

How do free and Pro work?

Start free with 3 notes a day, up to 3 minutes each. Go Pro for unlimited notes, recordings up to 3 hours, PDF export, advanced templates, custom vocabulary, every follow-up email tone, and full backup & restore — with a one-time lifetime unlock or a subscription.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored on your device. You can delete notes and recordings in the app (Settings → All recordings), or delete the app to remove locally stored data.
