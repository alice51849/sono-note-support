How to turn voice notes into clean meeting notes on iPhone

The meeting is over, the class has ended, and your notes are blank. Raw transcripts do not help much either: a wall of text is still something you have to read again. This guide shows a simple workflow to transcribe what was said and turn it into usable meeting notes, and how Sono Note turns a voice note into a summary, key points, action items and a follow-up email draft, with the original recording a tap away.

Step by step: from voice note to finished note

  1. Pick a template. Ready-made templates cover work updates, client calls, classes, interviews, doctor visits, ideas, support cases and project recaps.
  2. Record or just talk. Capture a thought, a lecture, a client call or a quick update. Clear, single-speaker recordings give the clearest results.
  3. Let it transcribe and tidy up. Sono Note transcribes the audio and hands you a finished note: summary, key points, action items and a follow-up email.
  4. Review the text. Generated text is meant to be reviewed before use; replay the original recording to check anything important.
  5. Export and share. Export as text, Markdown or PDF and share it anywhere.

Privacy, Local Only mode and backups

Your notes live on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking, and no servers of the developer's own, so nothing is uploaded to the developer. Transcription uses Apple's speech recognition, which may send audio to Apple for some languages; choose Local Only mode and everything stays on your device, even in airplane mode. You can back up everything to a single dated file and restore it in one tap, and browse, replay, share or delete every recording from one place.

Frequently asked questions

Can Sono Note transcribe a whole meeting?

Without a purchase each recording can be up to 3 minutes; the one-time in-app purchase allows recordings up to 3 hours.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Choose Local Only mode and everything stays on your device, even in airplane mode.

Is my audio uploaded anywhere?

Nothing is uploaded to the developer. Apple's speech recognition may send audio to Apple for some languages unless you use Local Only mode.

Does it work for lectures and interviews?

Yes. There are templates for classes and interviews, as well as work updates, client calls and doctor visits.

Can I export my notes?

Yes, as text or Markdown; PDF export is included with the one-time purchase.

Is Sono Note a subscription?

No. It is a single one-time in-app purchase with no subscription.

Sono Note is an independent app and is not affiliated with Apple. Transcription uses Apple's speech recognition, which may send audio to Apple for some languages unless you choose Local Only mode. Generated text is meant to be reviewed before use. Ask for consent before recording other people.
