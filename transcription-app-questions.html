Transcription app for meeting notes: questions before you download

If you want to record a meeting or lecture on iPhone and end up with notes you can actually use, Sono Note transcribes the recording and turns it into a summary, key points and an action-item list, with the original audio kept in the note. Here are the questions people ask most before downloading, answered from the App Store listing, including the limits.

What you get after a meeting

  • Transcript from Apple's speech recognition, in dozens of languages you can switch any time.
  • Summary, key points and action items instead of a wall of raw text.
  • Follow-up email draft in the tone you choose.
  • Templates for work updates, client calls, classes, interviews, doctor visits, ideas, support cases and project recaps.
  • The original recording stays in the note so you can replay and check anything.

Getting a clean transcript

The clearest results come from clear, single-speaker recordings. Put the iPhone close to whoever speaks most, avoid rooms with loud background noise, and pick the template that matches the conversation before you summarize. Generated text is meant to be reviewed before you send it to anyone.

Frequently asked questions

How long can a recording be?

Each recording can be up to 3 hours long, and recording and transcription are free with no daily limit.

Can it turn a meeting recording into notes with action items?

Yes. Record the meeting and Sono Note turns it into meeting notes with a clean summary, key points and an action-item list, and the original recording stays in the note so you can replay and check it. The meeting template helps you get a useful note on the first try; generated text is meant to be reviewed before you share it.

Does it summarize, or only transcribe?

Both. Sono Note turns the transcript into a clean summary, key points and an action-item list, and can draft a follow-up email. The free plan includes 2 AI summaries a day; every recording and transcript is saved, so you can summarize it later.

Is my audio uploaded?

Notes live on your iPhone with no account, ads or tracking, and the developer runs no servers of its own. Transcription uses Apple's speech recognition, which may send audio to Apple for some languages; choose Local Only mode and everything stays on your device, even in airplane mode.

Does it label different speakers?

Speaker labels are not listed among its features. It works best with clear, single-speaker recordings, so for a busy meeting place the phone near the main speaker and review the summary before sharing.

Can I export my notes?

You can export as text or Markdown, and Pro adds PDF export. You can also back up everything to a single dated file and restore it in one tap with Pro.

Is it a subscription?

No. Pro is a single one-time purchase that adds unlimited AI summaries, PDF export, advanced templates, custom vocabulary, every follow-up email tone, and full backup and restore.
