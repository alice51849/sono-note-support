Sono Note · Privacy

Data and privacy

Recordings, transcripts and generated notes stay on your device and are not sent to the developer. Apple Speech prefers on-device recognition when supported, but Apple may use network-based speech recognition for some languages, devices or modes.

Accounts, ads and tracking

No account is required. The app contains no third-party advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics.

Purchase

Core use is free to try. An optional one-time Pro purchase is handled by Apple; there is no subscription.

Deletion and recovery

Delete records from inside the app when that control is available. Deleting the app removes its local container; Apple-managed backups remain governed by your device settings.
