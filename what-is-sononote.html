What is Sono Note?

Sono Note is an iPhone app that records a meeting, lecture or quick idea, transcribes it and turns it into a clean summary, key points and an action-item list instead of a wall of raw transcript. It can draft a follow-up email in the tone you choose, keeps the original recording in the note so you can replay and check it, offers templates for meetings, client calls, classes, interviews and more, and exports text, Markdown or PDF. Generated text should be reviewed before use; clear, single-speaker recordings give the best results.

Who is Sono Note for?

Students recording lectures, people in meetings and client calls who need minutes and to-dos, interviewers, and anyone who thinks out loud and wants the idea written down. It also suits people who care about privacy: notes stay on the iPhone, there is no account, and a Local Only mode keeps everything on the device, even in airplane mode.

How does Sono Note charge?

Recording and transcription are free for everyone with no daily limit, and each recording can be up to 3 hours long. The free plan includes 2 AI summaries a day, and every recording and transcript is saved so you can summarize it later. A single one-time Pro purchase, with no subscription, adds unlimited AI summaries, PDF export, advanced templates, custom vocabulary, every follow-up email tone and full backup and restore. For the current price, check the App Store page.

How is Sono Note different from other transcription apps?

Based on what the listing describes: it gives a summary, key points and action items rather than only a transcript; it keeps the original audio next to the note; it can draft a follow-up email; the developer runs no servers, so nothing is uploaded to the developer (Apple speech recognition may send audio to Apple for some languages unless you choose Local Only); and Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
